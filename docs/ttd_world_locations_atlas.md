## 1. Crossroads Forward Fortress (크로스로드 / Forward Bastion)

### 1.1 Strategic Geography & The Great Rift Gorge
- **Geographic Placement**:
  - Crossroads is erected directly across the throat of the **Great Rift Gorge**—a titanic volcanic chasm that separates the fertile northern provinces of the Everblack Empire from the monster-infested abyssal badlands of the south.
  - The fortress sits atop sheer cliffs of compressed basalt and volcanic tuff. To the east and west, sheer mountain escarpments rise thousands of meters, making Crossroads the solitary terrestrial gateway into the empire.
  - If Crossroads falls, the monster hordes have an unhindered, downhill march into the undefended agrarian interior of the empire.

### 1.2 The Cyclopean Outer Bastion (30-Meter Basalt Curtain Walls)
- **Masonry & Metallurgy**:
  - The outer curtain wall stands **30 meters high** and **12 meters thick** at the walkway parapet, flaring outward to a 20-meter battered base to deflect battering rams and burrowing claws.
  - Constructed from monolithic, interlocking blocks of dark volcanic basalt (#2B2D31) and quarry-hewn iron-granite, bonded with ancient alchemical mortar impervious to non-magical siege strikes.
  - **Crenellations & Weapon Mounts**:
    - Heavy iron-banded stone merlons spaced every 2.5 meters provide cover for archers and arbalest crews.
    - Massive swivel-mounted twin-winch ballistas and scorpion siege engines line the upper towers.
    - Recessed iron pitch-vats (#121316) mounted on swiveling gantry cranes are pre-loaded with boiling naphtha to pour over scaling monstrosities.

### 1.3 The Kill-Zone Moat & Scorched Earth Perimeter
- **The Dry Trench**:
  - Directly beneath the outer wall lies a **50-meter-wide dry kill-zone moat** carved into the volcanic bedrock.
  - The moat floor is packed with charred wooden stakes, jagged iron caltrops, discarded tripwires linked to explosive alchemical pots, and concussive bomb craters from previous siege defenses.
  - The ground is perpetually scorched black by pitch fire, devoid of any vegetation, creating an absolute field of fire with zero cover for advancing beasts.

### 1.4 The Gatehouse, Funnel Portcullises & Murder Holes
- **Layered Defense in Depth**:
  - The main gateway consists of three successive gates arranged inside a narrow, 40-meter stone kill-corridor designed to funnel attacking hordes into a lethal choke point:
    1. *Outer Portcullis*: A grid of solid iron bars (15 cm thickness) operated by dual counterweights in the gatehouse towers.
    2. *The Murder Run*: A stone corridor with overhead murder holes through which garrison soldiers pour boiling pitch, quicklime, and drop explosive fire-crystals.
    3. *Inner Iron-Plated Oak Doors*: Massive double doors constructed from aged ironwood timber reinforced with crossed bands of cold-hammered steel, held fast by three sliding transverse locking beams.

### 1.5 The Middle District & Garrison City
- **The Civilian & Military Support Hub**:
  - Sheltered within the inner ring of walls lies the lower city of Crossroads—a claustrophobic labyrinth of steep, rain-slick cobblestone streets, soot-covered masonry, and timber-framed houses.
  - **Key Infrastructure**:
    - *The Forges of Vulcan*: Dozens of continuous blacksmith forges where armorsmiths repair shattered breastplates, re-tip crossbow bolts, and temper broadswords around the clock.
    - *The Alchemical Dispensary*: Smelling of sulfur, nitric acid, and dried herbs, where healing salves and incendiary pitch mixtures are brewed.
    - *The Last Drop Tavern & Soldier Barracks*: Crowded, noisy establishments where mercenaries, conscripts, and disgraced knights drink away the paralyzing terror of the night bells.

### 1.6 The Lord's Castle & Tactical Command Spire
- **The Architectural Apex**:
  - Rising at the northernmost and highest promontory of the fortress keep is the **Commander's Spire**.
  - Built from dark ashlar granite, the spire towers 60 meters above the fortress courtyards, crowned with a wide, stone-balustraded open command balcony.
  - **Tactical Utility**:
    - Provides Prince Ash with an unobstructed 360-degree panoramic view of the entire fortress perimeter, the kill-zone moat, and the roiling front of the Black Fog.
    - Equipped with signal braziers, acoustic speaking tubes connected to the gatehouse, and a large slate war table where tactical maps and unit tokens are manipulated during battle.

### 1.7 The Subterranean Dungeon Core & Ancient Seals
- **The Under-Fortress**:
  - Directly beneath the central keep, descending into the cold volcanic roots of the mountain, lies the **Subterranean Dungeon**.
  - An ancient, pre-imperial megalithic labyrinth sealed with massive bronze doors inscribed with runic binding wards.
  - **Strategic Significance**:
    - Houses the dormant dungeon core that anchors the fortress's protective barrier fields.
    - Serves as the high-risk harvesting ground where Ash dispatches elite squads to mine magical mana-stones, monster cores, and ancient relic weapons necessary to sustain the garrison’s defense.

## 2. The Black Fog (검은 안개 / The Abyssal Miasma & Monster World)

### 2.1 Physical Manifestation & The 300-Meter Boundary
- **The Southern Sky-Wall**:
  - The Black Fog (#090A0D) is a supernatural, roiling wall of impenetrable abyssal miasma that permanently marks the southern frontier of the known world.
  - It maintains an ominous, stable perimeter roughly **300 meters south** of the Crossroads kill-zone moat, rising vertically like a colossal black thunderstorm front that reaches thousands of meters into the upper stratosphere.
  - The boundary between the barren volcanic rock of the 300-meter dead zone and the roiling mist is knife-sharp: stepping one foot across the threshold plunges an observer into total, suffocating darkness.

### 2.2 Supernatural Properties: Light Absorption, Corrosive Toxicity & Scrying Nullification
- **Absolute Light Devouring**:
  - The Black Fog does not merely obscure vision; it actively consumes photons. Torchlight, magical luminescence, and solar rays dissolve within two meters of entering the mist.
  - Projectiles carrying burning pitch or incendiary alchemical agents are extinguished almost instantly upon crossing the fog threshold.
- **Atmospheric Toxicity & Psychological Terror**:
  - The mist is dense with abyssal spores and corrosive miasma. Inhaling the fog causes rapid hemorrhaging of lung tissue, severe nausea, and acute sensory hallucinations within minutes.
  - Unprotected soldiers stationed on the parapets must wear oiled leather breathing filters during southern wind surges to prevent miasmic poisoning.
- **Total Scrying & Aerial Reconnaissance Nullification**:
  - The arcane density of the fog disrupts all magical divination, aerial familiar scrying, and far-sight spells.
  - Teleportation into the fog invariably results in spatial fragmentation. Scout birds sent over the perimeter fall dead within seconds.
  - Consequently, Crossroads operates with zero early-warning radar: the garrison never knows the size, composition, or tier of an oncoming horde until the war horns sound and the beasts physically burst from the fog line into the 300-meter dead zone.

### 2.3 The Infinite Spawning Engine of the Monster Realm
- **The Planar Maw**:
  - Beyond the Black Fog lies the Monster World—a twisted, non-Euclidean wilderness of corrupted ecosystems, subterranean hives, and abyssal breeding pits.
  - The fog acts as a physical dimensional conduit, continuously coalescing raw chaotic mana into biological killing machines.
  - As the campaign advances from Stage 0 to Stage 50, the fog unleashes progressively deadlier echelons of horrors: from simple arachnid scavengers to gargoyle swarms, armored minotaurs, bone-armored siege behemoths, and ultimately apocalyptic disaster-class demon lords.

## 3. The Everblack Empire & The Imperial Capital (New Terra / 뉴 테라)

### 3.1 Geopolitical Hierarchy & The Bloodline of Dragons
- **The Hegemony of the North**:
  - The Everblack Empire is the undisputed superpower of the continent, holding dominion over hundreds of vassal principalities, duchies, and free cities.
  - The ruling dynasty claims direct descent from ancient primordial dragons, an ancestry manifested in their superhuman physical constitution and innate affinity for devastating draconic magic.
  - Despite its military might, the empire is spiritually hollow: decades of unbroken luxury have rendered the central aristocracy decadent, corrupt, and obsessed with petty court status.

### 3.2 The Architecture of New Terra: White Marble & Golden Spires
- **The City of Light**:
  - Situated thousands of kilometers north in fertile temperate river plains, New Terra stands in glaring aesthetic and moral contrast to the grime of Crossroads.
  - Built from blindingly white Carrara marble, polished limestone, and gold-leafed domes that catch the northern sun.
  - **Key Landmarks**:
    - *The Imperial Palace of Dragons*: A sprawling city-within-a-city featuring sweeping colonnades, crystal chandeliers, manicured botanical labyrinths, and artificial water fountains.
    - *The High Arcane Tower*: A soaring needle of blue-veined white marble where imperial arch-mages conduct research into forbidden planar energies while deliberately ignoring the southern crisis.
    - *The Golden Boulevard*: Broad cobblestone thoroughfares illuminated day and night by enchanted luminescent crystals, along which noble carriages line the opera houses and fine dining salons.

### 3.3 The Cutthroat Succession Game of the 36 Heirs
- **The Throne of Blood**:
  - The reigning Emperor has fathered **36 recognized royal princes and princesses** across multiple imperial consorts and concubines.
  - Imperial law does not observe strict primogeniture; rather, the throne will pass to whichever heir demonstrates the greatest personal power, political ruthlessness, and factional dominance.
  - The capital is a hotbed of covert poisonings, arranged assassinations, manufactured scandals, and mercenary skirmishes as royal factions vie for imperial favor.

### 3.4 The Imperial Center's Disdain for the Southern Front
- **The Discarded Shield**:
  - To the haughty courtiers of New Terra, the southern frontier is a vulgar, barbaric wasteland. Crossroads is viewed not as the empire’s vital shield, but as an open-air penal colony.
  - Soldiers assigned to Crossroads are written off as dead men. Requests for food shipments, stonemasons, gunpowder, or magical armaments sent by the fortress commander are routinely ignored or delayed by imperial bureaucrats.
  - When Prince Ash was exiled to Crossroads, the court celebrated: they assumed the "Trash Prince" would be devoured by monsters within a month, cleanly removing one more claimant from the succession board without leaving blood on their hands.

## 4. The Lake Kingdom (호수 왕국 / The Sunken Realm — Zones 5 through 10)

### 4.1 The Ancient Fallen Civilization
- **The Drowned Empire**:
  - Located deep beyond the southern perimeter, submerged beneath a subterranean abyssal inland sea, lies the **Sunken Lake Kingdom**.
  - Long before the rise of the Everblack Empire, this was a magnificent realm of advanced arcane architecture, crystalline aqueducts, and high-elven civilization.
  - Following the cataclysmic rupture of the planar boundary, the kingdom was swallowed by black waters and sealed beneath the earth, becoming the late-game expedition theater (Campaign Stages 30 through 50, categorized as Zones 5 through 10).

### 4.2 Environmental Aesthetic: Perpetual Twilight & Submerged Cathedrals
- **The Visual Palette**:
  - Saturated in eerie perpetual twilight: cold abyssal midnight blues (#0F1722), phosphorescent cyan waters (#00E5FF), and the sickly violet glow of deep-sea witch-fire (#4A154B).
  - Crumbling gothic cathedrals, soaring marble arches, and shattered colonnades jutting out from still, mirror-like black waters.
  - Silent, flooded courtyards where the only sound is the rhythmic dripping of condensation from colossal cavern stalactites.
  - Submerged bridges lined with weathered statues of forgotten deities whose stone faces have been worn smooth by centuries of dark currents.

### 4.3 The Immortal Curse & The Sleepless Princess of the Lake
- **The Curse of Endless Drowning**:
  - The citizens and royal guard of the Lake Kingdom did not perish during the cataclysm; instead, they were bound by an unholy curse of perpetual resurrection.
  - When slain, their water-bloated corpses and spectral forms dissolve into mist, only to reassemble hours later within the drowned crypts. They exist in an eternal state of agony, unable to rest, compelled to slaughter any living expedition that enters their flooded halls.
- **The Climax Threat: The Sleepless Princess (The Nameless One)**:
  - Presiding over the deepest sunken throne room is the tragic final sovereign of the realm: **The Sleepless Princess of the Lake** (also known in imperial records as *The Nameless One*).
  - Clad in tattered bridal silk that floats weightlessly in the water, she commands apocalyptic hydro-kinetic magic and necrotic soul-reaping arts.
  - In Tae-Seok's 1,056th run, she served as the catastrophic penultimate barrier that wiped out his elite strike team before Lucas delivered the decisive blow.

## 5. Evergreen (에버그린 / The Ancient Elven Heartland)

### 5.1 The Primeval Forest & Colossal Ironwood World Trees
- **The Sacred East**:
  - Stretching for thousands of leagues across the eastern flank of the continent lies **Evergreen**—the untouched ancestral homeland of the High Elves.
  - Dominated by colossal ironwood World Trees whose trunks measure hundreds of meters in diameter and whose dense, multi-tiered canopies completely block out the sun, casting the forest floor in perpetual emerald twilight.
  - The air is thick with luminescent fungal spores, hanging moss, and crystalline mana springs where raw magical essence wells up directly from the deep veins of the earth.

### 5.2 High Elven Society: Isolationism, Sacred Mana Springs & Bowcraft
- **The Xenophobic Hegemony**:
  - High Elven civilization is ruled by ancient clan matriarchs and ranger lords who regard human civilization with deep suspicion and open condescension.
  - They view the Everblack Empire as a parasitic, short-sighted empire whose unchecked expansion provoked the awakening of the Black Fog.
  - **Martial Prowess**:
    - The elves are peerless masters of wind-arbalest archery, spirit-communion magic, and silent forest skirmishing.
    - Their compound bows and arbalests, crafted from living ironwood boughs and strung with basilisk sinew, can pierce steel breastplates at distances exceeding 400 meters.

### 5.3 Diplomatic Fragility with the Everblack Empire
- **The Cold Peace**:
  - Evergreen maintains an armed, fragile non-aggression treaty with the Empire, guarded by fortified border watchtowers.
  - The elves strictly refuse to send military aid or archers to the Southern Front, choosing to shelter behind their primordial boundary wards.
  - Criminals, political dissenters, and elves who commit taboo blood magic or murder within the sacred forest are stripped of their clan names, branded with burning silver, and exiled beyond the border—frequently drifting south toward Crossroads.

## 6. The Shadow Squad / Shadow Guild (그림자 부대)

### 6.1 Origins & Composition: Disgraced Elven Convict Outcasts
- **The Expendables of the Frontier**:
  - Assigned directly to Prince Ash upon his exile to Crossroads is the **Shadow Squad** (그림자 부대)—a deniable, penal black-ops unit composed entirely of disgraced High Elven death-row convicts and outcasts.
  - Stripped of their names, cast out of Evergreen, and facing execution in imperial dungeons, they were offered a singular condition by imperial decree: serve as cannon fodder on the southern frontier until death claims them.

### 6.2 The Operatives & Specializations
The squad comprises five highly specialized, lethal killers:
1. **Godhand (신손 / *Shin-Son*)**:
   - Master of mechanical traps, lock-picking, and silent wire garroting. A disgraced elven craftsman who stole sacred artifacts from the Evergreen vault.
2. **Body Bag (시체포대 / *Si-Che-Po-Dae*)**:
   - The muscular enforcer. Unlike typical slender elves, possesses dense physical muscle mass, wielding heavy serrated iron cleavers and trench daggers in brutal, close-quarters combat.
3. **Old Girl (할매 / *Hal-Mae*)**:
   - The veteran sniper. A cold, cynical scout whose compound bow never misses, specialized in hunting high-value targets from extreme range with poisoned silver arrows.
4. **Skull (해골 / *Hae-Gol*)**:
   - Infiltration and espionage specialist. Possesses uncanny anatomical knowledge, capable of moving through ventilation culverts and dissecting monster corpses to extract intact mana cores.
5. **Burnout (탈진 / *Tal-Jin*)**:
   - Volatile alchemical sapper and pyromaniac. Specialized in formulating unstable incendiary fire-bombs and laying explosive minefields in the 300-meter dead zone.

### 6.3 Black-Ops Doctrine: Night Reconnaissance, Subterranean Sabotage & Commander Assassination
- **Operating Beyond the Shield**:
  - The Shadow Squad does not stand upon the ramparts with the city guard. Their domain is the dark, rotting earth outside the walls.
  - Under cover of night, they slip through hidden drainage sally-ports into the 300-meter dead zone and the subterranean tunnels connecting Crossroads to the ancient ruins.
  - **Tactical Mission Profile**:
    - Mapping advancing monster horde formations inside the periphery of the Black Fog.
    - Rigging subterranean cave-ins with alchemical charges to collapse monster staging areas.
    - Conducting deep-penetration assassination strikes against high-tier monster commanders and brood mothers, disorganizing the horde before it reaches the moat.

### 6.4 The Pact with Prince Ash: Mutual Pragmatic Allegiance
- **Command Through Ruthless Pragmatism**:
  - Previous imperial commanders treated the Shadow Squad with contempt, using them as expendable bait and sending them to their deaths.
  - Prince Ash operates differently. Drawing on his gamer mindset, he recognizes their extraordinary utility value. He does not moralize or demand chivalric loyalty; instead, he offers an unvarnished mercenary contract:
    - Absolute tactical autonomy outside the walls.
    - First access to black-market rations, alchemical supplies, and high-tier weapons salvaged from dungeon runs.
    - A guarantee that their lives will never be thrown away casually—every mission Ash assigns them is calculated with geometric precision to ensure maximum survival probability.
  - In return, the cynical convicts yield cold, ruthless obedience to Ash’s commands, becoming the invisible scalpel to Knight Lucas’s unbreakable shield.
