## 1. Executive Summary & Creative Mandate

Episode 1 of *Tyrant of the Tower Defense Game* establishes the dramatic and visual foundation of the entire animated series. The pilot accomplishes a high-stakes tonal and stylistic transition:
1. **The Game Climax (Prologue)**: Lee Tae-Seok, a reclusive strategy gaming ascetic, achieves the global first completion of the impossible dark-fantasy tower defense simulation *Protect the Empire* on Hell Mode Ironman. His victory is pyrrhic: 99.8% of his roster has been annihilated, leaving only his veteran vanguard anchor, Knight Lucas MacGregor, standing alone in a ruined fortress before collapsing from physical and emotional exhaustion.
2. **The Metaphysical Bridge**: The match-cut transition from the pitch-black monitor void into the freezing, sulfurous reality of Crossroads Forward Bastion.
3. **The Living Rebirth**: Tae-Seok awakens inside the body of Prince Ash Bornhater, the despised 36th imperial heir banished to the southern penal front. Here, permadeath is not a deleted save file but living human flesh being shredded by monsters.
4. **The Tutorial Crisis**: Before he can orient himself, the war horns of Stage 0 scream across the ramparts as abyssal horrors breach the outer drainage ditch. Reunited with an unawakened, Level 1 version of Lucas who regards him with contempt, Ash must cast aside the gamer's detachment and become the ruthless tyrant commander necessary to keep his soldiers alive.

### Visual Hybridization Principle
The visual signature combines four premier animation traditions into a cohesive dark-fantasy aesthetic:
- **Fortiche Arcane**: Rich tactile oil/gouache impasto brushwork, sculpted 3D facial planes, bold graphic contours, and volumetric chiaroscuro rim lighting.
- **Manhwa Grit**: Visceral emotional intensity, diagonal composition tension, desperate survival expressions, and authentic battle fatigue.
- **Ufotable Materiality & Physics**: Physical inertia of blackened steel plate armor, authentic metal roughness and light bounce, zero weightless float, and grounded particle physics.
- **Wit Studio / MAPPA Kinetic Virtual Camera**: Multi-plane parallax tracking with rapid linear acceleration during strikes, locked perspective vanishing points, and atmospheric focal depth.

## 2. Full 12-Scene Episodic Narrative Arc

### Scene 01: The Endurance Vigil (Officetel Setup)
- **Setting**: Cramped, unventilated one-room officetel in Seoul. Low cyan glow from a dual-monitor rig, acoustic foam tiles taped to cracked wallpaper, tangled braided cables, crusted instant ramyeon bowls, empty taurine energy drink cans.
- **Narrative Beat**: Classic Gamer Geek (Lee Tae-Seok, age 23) sits in an ergonomic chair polished smooth from millions of clicks. It is the final seconds of his 1,056th Hell Mode Ironman run across three grueling years. The chat counter ticks silently as thousands of lurkers watch with held breath.
- **Visual Anchor**: Extreme close-up on bloodshot pupils reflecting scrolling damage logs and keyboard keystrokes at 420 APM.

### Scene 02: The Bastion of Ruin (Stage 50 Breach & Zone 10 Sunken Cathedral Wipe)
- **Setting**: Flashback cut of the shattered gothic cathedral boss arena (`bg_cathedral_boss_arena.png`) from the penultimate campaign barrier against the Sleepless Princess, transitioning into the pulverized 30m basalt ramparts under freezing slanting rain and lightning.
- **Narrative Beat**: The battlefield aftermath of the 50-stage campaign attrition. The Black Fog has swallowed the outer defenses. Fallen heroes litter the rain-slicked flagstones: Evangeline unconscious beside her shattered golden aegis and lance, and blind wind-sniper Lily slumped beside her broken composite arbalest.
- **Visual Anchor**: High-angle crane shot sweeping across the flooded flagstone floor where dark crimson blood swirls with rain puddles.

### Scene 03: The Solitary Bulwark (Lucas Vanguard Stand — Cut `CL-01`)
- **Setting**: The breach in the central basalt bastion (`bg_crossroads_ramparts_night.png`). Boiling pitch fires sizzle in the rain.
- **Narrative Beat**: Against the colossal silhouette of the final demon wave, Knight Lucas MacGregor steps to the apex of the last surviving four-man vanguard squad (`99.8%` casualties pending). His *Man of Steel* trait locks his life at 1 HP; his *Strike of Will* stacks reach maximum physical tension.
- **Visual Anchor**: Low-angle 3/4 medium shot of Lucas's battered silhouette (`CL-01`), bastard sword held at high guard, rain dripping from his notched blade.

### Scene 04: The Command Roar Climax (The 4-Shot Vanguard Sequence — Cut `CL-02` to `CL-04`)
- **Setting**: The smoking bastion breach (`bg_crossroads_ramparts_night.png`).
- **Narrative Beat**: Lucas leads the final desperate counter-charge where the three auxiliary spearmen sacrifice themselves to buy cooldown frames. Framed across the climax sequence (`CL-01` established in `SHOT-03`, `CL-02` 4.0s roar push-in in `SHOT-04`, `CL-03` foreshortened charge, and `CL-04` terminal dark void transition into `SHOT-05`), he unhinges his jaw into a roaring command ("CHARGE!") before standing as the sole survivor in the dark void.
- **Visual Anchor**: The 3/4 Medium Close-Up of Lucas's roaring face with three charging vanguard spearmen surging forward in background bokeh (`SHOT-04` / `CL-02`).

### Scene 05: Pyrrhic Victory & Gamer Collapse
- **Setting**: Back inside the dimly lit Seoul officetel (`bg_streamer_room_night.png`).
- **Narrative Beat**: The final boss disintegrates into black ash. The primary gaming monitor flashes violently: `[ STAGE 50: CLEAR! ]`, `[ CASUALTIES: 99.8% ]`. Chat erupts in donation fireworks. But Tae-Seok drops his mouse. His hands tremble uncontrollably. He stares at the monitor where Lucas stands alone in the rain with bowed head, back turned to the screen (`CL-04`). Tae-Seok collapses forward, his forehead hitting the keyboard as he weeps: *"We won... but at what cost? Everyone is dead."* Exhaustion overwhelms his body, plunging him into black unconsciousness.
- **Visual Anchor**: Over-the-shoulder shot of the hunched gamer weeping against the keyboard, the screen casting cold cyan light across his shaking shoulders.

### Scene 06: The Void & Sensory Awakening
- **Setting**: Transitional weightless abyss dissolving into a drafty, cavernous stone bedchamber.
- **Narrative Beat**: Tae-Seok regains consciousness not to the hum of PC fans, but to sensory shock: the freezing mountain wind whistling through arrow slits, the pungent stench of tallow candles, damp moldy granite, horse manure, and sulfur from volcanic vents. He gasps for air, shivering on a four-poster bed draped in faded imperial crimson velvet.
- **Visual Anchor**: Shallow focus macro shot of his trembling hand gripping coarse wool sheets, feeling real tactile fabric and cold stone dust instead of plastic keycaps.

### Scene 07: The Mirror of the 36th Prince
- **Setting**: The dilapidated Lord's Keep chamber of Crossroads Fortress. Peat embers dying in a massive stone hearth.
- **Narrative Beat**: Stumbling across freezing flagstones, Tae-Seok stands before a tall, cracked silvered mirror. Looking back is not his exhausted 23-year-old face, but the aristocratic, sickly pale visage of Prince Ash Bornhater: sharp jawline, bruised-purple insomnia rings, disheveled black hair, predatory amber-gold irises, and an uncut obsidian signet ring. The reality of transmigration crashes over him: he is trapped in the hated 36th prince exiled to die.
- **Visual Anchor**: Symmetrical medium shot into the cracked mirror reflection, an amber spark catching in Ash's eye as the gamer's razor intellect awakens behind royal flesh.

### Scene 08: The Threshold Encounter (The Living Lucas)
- **Setting**: The drafty flagstone corridor outside the Lord's bedchamber.
- **Narrative Beat**: Clasping his aching head, Ash opens the heavy oak door. Standing vigil against the wall is Knight Lucas MacGregor. But the dynamic is inverted: Lucas stands with cold, contemptuous rigidity, guarding his lord out of chivalric oath to House MacGregor while despising Ash's cowardly imperial reputation. To Ash, however, the young man before him is the holy brother-in-arms of 1,056 sacrificial campaigns.
- **Visual Anchor**: Reverse over-the-shoulder shot: Lucas looking down with guarded, icy eyes at Prince Ash, who stares back with unmasked emotional recognition.

### Scene 09: The Diagnostic Shock (The Living Roster)
- **Setting**: The corridor threshold. Torches flicker along damp stone brackets.
- **Narrative Beat**: Ash's gamer diagnostic vision instinctively activates. Floating semi-transparent stat brackets register over Lucas: `[ Level: 1 ]`, `[ Rank: SSR (Base Unawakened) ]`, `[ Man of Steel: Dormant ]`. Panic seizes Ash: this is not his god-slaying, 1000-kill veteran; this is an unblooded greenhorn knight with basic steel armor and an unnotched blade. If he steps into battle carelessly, he will die instantly—and in this reality, permadeath means permanent physical rotting in the mud.
- **Visual Anchor**: Close-up of Ash's dilated golden pupil reflecting faint blue status UI lines that dissolve into the damp stone texture of Lucas's armor.

### Scene 10: The Alarm of Stage 0 (The Southern Horns)
- **Setting**: The panoramic fortress battlements overlooking the 300m dead zone and the Great Rift Gorge.
- **Narrative Beat**: The silence of dawn is shattered by the deafening wail of bronze siren bells and brass war horns. On the southern horizon, the Black Fog churns into a blood-purple tempest. Screams echo from the lower courtyards: Tier-1 arachnid horrors (Black Crawlers and Corpse Scavengers) have scaled the dry moat and breached the drainage culverts. Tutorial Stage 0 has begun.
- **Visual Anchor**: Extreme wide establishing shot of Crossroads' 30-meter cyclopean walls, pitch smoke rising from braziers as dozens of black, multi-limbed horrors boil over the outer ditch.

### Scene 11: Chaos on the Ramparts (Garrison Panic)
- **Setting**: The breached middle-district gatehouse.
- **Narrative Beat**: Absolute bedlam: conscript levies throw down their spears and flee; mercenary captains lock themselves in taverns; junior officers scream contradictory orders. Lucas draws his broadsword with desperate resolve, preparing to step forward alone into a hopeless rearguard choke point to die for a fleeing garrison.
- **Visual Anchor**: Tracking shot following Lucas's determined strides through a stampede of fleeing soldiers, his steel greaves kicking through dropped iron helmets.

### Scene 12: The Tyrant's Command (The First Defense Begins)
- **Setting**: The bottleneck stone archway of the middle district.
- **Narrative Beat**: Ash bursts onto the wooden walkway above the gate. Suppressing all fear, he taps into the cold geometric calculations of 1,056 runs. He screams a command so sharp, arrogant, and authoritative that it halts the fleeing soldiers in their tracks: *"Draw your blade, Knight Lucas! If you want to die like a coward, step aside. If you want to live, put your shield on that doorway and do not move an inch until I tell you to breathe!"* Lucas hesitates, stunned by the prince's terrifying tactical certainty, before slamming his iron-reinforced pavise into the flagstones. The defense of Crossroads begins.
- **Visual Anchor**: Low-angle heroic two-shot: Ash standing on the parapet against the blood-purple sky pointing downward with his obsidian ring, while below him Lucas braces his massive tower shield into the stone, blocking the first wave of claws.

## 3. Master Character Model Sheet: Knight Lucas MacGregor

### 3.1 Core Identity & Archetype
- **Name & Title**: Lucas MacGregor (루카스 맥그리거) — Vanguard Knight of House MacGregor, Escort Knight to Prince Ash Everblack, later known as *The Night Closer* (밤을 닫는 자).
- **Archetype**: The Immovable Shield / The Tragic Loyalist / "The Golden Retriever Knight."
- **Age**: 22–25 (Young prime during Stage 0/1; battle-hardened veteran in later stages).
- **Physique**: Muscular powerhouse (~192 cm / 6'3.5"). Wide, dense trapezius and shoulder girdles built to absorb multi-ton kinetic impacts from charging beasts; heavily calloused hands scarred by grip wire.
- **Psychological Profile**: Unshakable chivalric honor, deep empathy beneath a stern countenance, and chronic survivor's guilt. Believes every casualty on his watch is a personal failure.

### 3.2 Cranial & Facial Master Anatomy
- **Bone Architecture**: Heavy square jawline, pronounced zygomatic arches, wide mandibular angle, and a broad chin with a subtle vertical cleft. Deep supraorbital brow ridge casting natural ambient occlusion shadows over the eye sockets.
- **Glabella & Stress Furrows**: Twin vertical furrows (corrugator supercilii contraction) deeply carved between the brows from continuous vigilance and siege stress.
- **Hair**: Dirty-blonde / honey-amber (#C49A45 base, #5A401E shadow crevices, #E8C170 highlight glints), heavily dulled by soot, rain, and dried blood. Shaggy layered cut falling to chin length in front; thick, wet clumps whip dynamically across his forehead.
- **Skin Texture & Tone**: Coarse, weathered bronze-tan skin (#C29672) with visible pore texture along the cheekbones and nose bridge. Fine sweat beads and rain droplets glistening along the jaw plane.
- **Combat Scars & Battle Detailing**:
  - *The Left Cheek Claw Scar*: Signature three-furrow healed keloid scar (#B88A8A) running diagonally from his left high cheekbone across his jawline.
  - *Fresh Battle Laceration*: 25mm laceration along the right temple hairline oozing a sluggish trickle of dark venous blood (#4A0E17) that mixes with rainwater down his right mandibular plane.
  - *Soot & Grime*: Dark charcoal soot smudges (#222226) across his forehead and temple.

### 3.3 Eyes & Gaze Direction (Strict Grounded Realism Invariant)
- **Iris Morphology**: Natural weathered steel-gray irises (#4A5A66 outer limbal ring, #708494 inner stroma) with subtle slate-blue undertones.
- **Catchlights & Reflections**: Strict physical realism—irises feature twin specular reflections: a warm flickering amber glint at the 10 o'clock position (reflecting rampart pitch braziers) and a sharp, cold slate-blue rim highlight at 4 o'clock (reflecting the twilight storm horizon).
- **Pupils & Sclera**: Dilated pupils (~5.5mm) under acute adrenaline surge. Sclera exhibits micro-capillary engorgement (bloodshot redness) at the outer canthi from 48 hours of smoke inhalation, windburn, and sleep deprivation.
- **STRICT PROHIBITION**: ZERO glowing eyes, ZERO laser pupils, ZERO floating sparks, ZERO blue lightning arcs around armor, ZERO flaming magical runes on sword.

### 3.4 Armor Metallurgy & Textile Heraldry
- **Harness Archetype**: Heavy 15th-century gothic plate steel harness with hand-hammered planar facets and an oiled matte gunmetal finish (#26292E base, #484E58 specular edges, #121316 ambient occlusion crevices). Zero mirror chrome or clean parade gloss.
- **Battle Patina & Wear**: Two deep parallel claw gouges (3mm depth) torn across the left breastplate and left pauldron, exposing raw unoxidized silver-white steel (#D8DDE6) with raised metal burrs. Mottled black acid-etching on the gorget; concave hammer dents on the breastplate plackart.
- **Fasteners**: Hand-forged domed iron rivets with faint rings of rust (#5C3826); dark brown bridle leather straps (#3A2418) with iron wire-sewn buckles and frayed ends.
- **Underlayers**: Riveted round-wire iron maille hauberk visible at the neck hollow beneath the gorget and along the armpit voids.
- **Heraldic Tabard**: Heavy coarse-weave wool surcoat dyed faded imperial navy (#1E2D48), split down front and back. Emblazoned with the House MacGregor lion paw and Crossroads fortress tower crest—blackened by soot, frayed at the hem, and stained with dark dried blood (#381014).

### 3.5 Weaponry & Combat Mechanics
- **Vanguard Bastard Sword (Oakeshott Type XVIIIb)**: ~90 cm high-carbon steel blade with diamond cross-section, central fuller extending down the proximal third. Cutting edges display four distinct micro-notches from striking monster bone and armor. Blackened steel crossguard with downturned octagonal quillons; hexagonal scent-stopper pommel; dark oxblood cord-wrapped grip (#2A1816).
- **Spiked Pavise (Tower Shield)**: ~140 cm tall, 65 cm wide iron-reinforced oak pavise with heavy central iron boss and hardened lower ground-spikes that bite into stone flagstones to resist charging beasts.
- **Core Mechanics**: *Man of Steel* (Lethal damage nullification at 1 HP), *Strike of Will* (Damage scaling per kill), *Step of Tenacity* (Kinetic spatial intercept).

### 3.6 Signature Dialogue Lines
- *"My Lord, wherever your command points, my shield will follow. Even unto the end of the world."*
- *"As long as I draw breath, not a single beast shall set foot upon this wall!"*
- *"Do not falter! Look at the Commander! Hold the line!"*

## 6. Cinematography Master Guide: The 4-Shot Climax Sequence

The Climax Sequence directly resolves the user's creative feedback on Knight Lucas's vanguard scene. The camera moves from static isolation into dynamic 3/4 vanguard command authority:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| SHOT CL-01 (00:00-00:02.5) | SHOT CL-02 (00:02.5-00:06.5) | SHOT CL-03 (00:06.5-00:08.5) | SHOT CL-04 (00:08.5-00:12.0) |
| Establishing Vanguard Step | The Command Roar (3/4 CU)    | Kinetic Vanguard Charge Cut | Solitary Back in Dark Void  |
| 35mm Handheld Low MCU      | 50mm Dutch Tilt 12 deg Push  | 24mm Extreme Foreshortening | 85mm Locked Telephoto Rear  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 6.1 Shot CL-01: The Breached Bastion (Establishing 3/4 Low MCU)
- **Duration**: 2.50 seconds (60 frames @ 24 fps CFR, 1280x720).
- **Camera Vector**: 35mm wide-normal focal length, 3/4 low angle (Yaw +30 deg, Pitch +10 deg). Subtle handheld camera micro-shake mimicking a combat documentarian in the trench.
- **Composition**: Lucas occupies the right-third vertical grid line, stepping over shattered volcanic basalt blocks. Screen-left reveals the smoking breach in the curtain wall, torrential 35-degree slanting rain, and distant lightning flashes over the Great Rift Gorge.
- **Temporal Action Progression**:
  - `0.0s - 1.2s`: Wind whips his frayed navy tabard. Lucas transfers his center of mass forward, planting his heavy iron greave into the water-logged gravel with a concussive crunch.
  - `1.2s - 2.5s`: He pivots his head sharply toward the breached gate, bastard sword gripped in his right gauntlet, shield lowered into guard.
- **Lighting Model**:
  - Key: Warm flickering brazier amber (#D96B27, 70% luminance) from screen-left.
  - Rim: Cold storm twilight slate-blue (#5C7A99, 45% luminance) along his pauldron edge.
  - Ambient: Volcanic dark ash (#121316).

### 6.2 Shot CL-02: The Command Roar (Core User-Critique Climax Cut)
- **Duration**: 4.00 seconds (96 frames @ 24 fps CFR, 1280x720).
- **Camera Vector**: 50mm portrait focal length, dynamic three-quarter (3/4) Medium Close-Up (MCU) pushing in ~15% along the optical Z-axis into a tight Close-Up (CU). Counter-clockwise Dutch tilt locked at 12 degrees.
- **Composition**: Lucas's roaring face dominates the center-right frame at Yaw 30 deg, chin raised, shouting orders across the battle line. Over his left shoulder in deep cinematic bokeh (f/2.2), the blurred silhouettes of three charging vanguard spearmen with raised iron spears and roaring torches surge forward.
- **Temporal Action Progression**:
  - `0.0s - 1.0s`: Lucas leans forward, jaw clenched tight, brow clamped hard, eyes locked on the breach. Masseter muscles flex prominently along his jaw hinge.
  - `1.0s - 2.5s`: Mandible drops open, lips peel back over bared canines, nostrils flare as his chest expands to inhale cold air.
  - `2.5s - 4.0s`: Full guttural battle roar ("CHARGE! / FORWARD!"). Condensation breath vapor and fine saliva droplets burst into the freezing rain. Neck tendons strain taut; camera push-in reaches tightest framing on his cheek scar and resolute steel-gray eye.
- **Lighting Model**:
  - Key: Roaring pitch brazier (#D96B27, 75% luminance) casting warm amber light on his left cheek and scar.
  - Rim: Cold midnight slate-blue (#5C7A99, 50% luminance) tracing jawline, hair clumps, and gunmetal pauldron rim.
  - Catchlight: Twin natural specular catchlights in his steel-gray irises (10 o'clock brazier, 4 o'clock horizon). Zero sparks.

### 6.3 Shot CL-03: The Kinetic Charge Cut (Manhwa Visceral Cut)
- **Duration**: 2.00 seconds (48 frames @ 24 fps CFR, 1280x720).
- **Camera Vector**: 24mm ultra-wide lens, low tracking shot moving at high linear velocity parallel to Lucas's charging strides.
- **Composition**: Extreme perspective foreshortening. The notched blade of his bastard sword extends toward the camera in the upper-right quadrant, cutting cleanly through suspended raindrops. Mud and rain splash violently off charging boots in the lower third.
- **Kinetic Action**: Lucas accelerates into full sprint, leading the vanguard line into the monster swarm. High-energy impact cut with zero camera float or weightlessness.

### 6.4 Shot CL-04: The Solitary Back in Dark Void (Narrative Match-Cut)
- **Duration**: 3.50 seconds (84 frames @ 24 fps CFR, 1280x720).
- **Camera Vector**: 85mm telephoto lens, locked low-angle rear shot centered squarely on Lucas's hunched broad shoulders.
- **Composition**: Symmetrical rear framing with high negative space.
- **Visual Action**: The devastated rampart, rain, and fallen soldiers gradually desaturate and dissolve into deep black shadow. Within 1.5 seconds, all background geometry dissolves into pure pitch-black abyss (#000000, bottom-third mean luminance <= 15.0). Lucas stands alone with his notched sword held tip-down in his right gauntlet, broad muscular shoulders hunched forward under overwhelming grief.
- **Narrative Bridge**: Direct visual match to Streamer Ash's monitor screen ("Everyone other than Lucas died... His back looked so lonely.").

## 7. Visual Hybridization Principles & Technical Synthesis

### 7.1 Quad-Discipline Synthesis Matrix
| Discipline | Aesthetic Contribution | Technical Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| **Fortiche Arcane** | Painterly impasto texture, bold graphic ink contours, sculpted 3D facial planes | Dual-source volumetric lighting (amber key vs slate rim), visible oil/gouache strokes on armor and skin planes |
| **Manhwa Grit** | Visceral emotional intensity, diagonal tension, desperate survival expressions | 12-degree Dutch tilts, extreme facial muscle contractions (corrugator, platysma), bared canines, heavy combat trauma |
| **Ufotable Physics** | Physical mass, plate armor inertia, authentic metal roughness | Zero weightless float, authentic kinetic momentum, natural specular reflections off battered carbon steel, real rain particle collision |
| **Wit / MAPPA Camera** | Virtual camera tracking, multi-plane parallax depth | 15% Z-axis push-in coupled with differential foreground/background parallax velocities, locked vanishing points |

### 7.2 Background Drift Suppression Architecture
To prevent the background warping noted during review (*"The background will constantly drift; it must be anchored back to canon"*), all scenes are bound to a 4-tier drift defense:
1. **Canonical Golden Plates (`output/canonical_plates/`)**: Frozen 16:9 master plates (`bg_crossroads_ramparts_night.png`, `bg_cathedral_boss_arena.png`, `bg_streamer_room_night.png`) providing immutable architectural and lighting palette truth across exterior and dark basalt keep scenes.
2. **Soft Gaussian ROI Masking ($M(x,y)$, $\sigma=28$ px)**: Active character action is isolated within an elliptical foreground mask; peripheral background pixels are locked to the ground truth plate ($\text{MAE}_{\text{bg}} \le 6.0$).
3. **Dual-Boundary Lock (`Mode.I2V`)**: Both start (`first_frame.png`) and end (`last_frame.png`) frames are registered with identical background perspective, preventing the diffusion sampler from hallucinating unrequested camera pans or morphing stone blocks.
4. **Programmatic Spark & Laplacian Filter**: Every generated keyframe is scanned for arcade artifacts; pixels in face/eye regions with $V > 245$ and $S > 120$ or extreme Laplacian variance ($>180$) are rejected.

## 8. Verification & Delivery Invariants

1. **Resolution & Aspect Ratio**: Exact 1280x720 progressive scan (16:9 widescreen).
2. **Framerate & Encoding**: 24.0 fps Constant Frame Rate (CFR), H.264 video codec with FastStart (`moov` atom preceding `mdat`), AAC audio stream.
3. **Linguistic Purity**: 100% English-only source code and documentation (zero raw Cyrillic in `.py` files; unicode escapes if localized strings are required).
4. **Artistic Grounding**: Absolute compliance with the zero-sparks rule for Knight Lucas and the blind-sniper condition for Lily.
