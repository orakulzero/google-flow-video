## 1. The Prologue & The Architecture of "Protect the Empire"

### 1.1 The Gamer: Lee Tae-Seok ("Classic Gamer Geek")
- **Real-World Identity**: Lee Tae-Seok (이태석), age 23. A reclusive, hyper-obsessive strategy prodigy and hardcore gaming ascetic living in a cramped, unventilated one-room officetel in the outskirts of Seoul, South Korea.
- **Physical Environment**:
  - The room is saturated in the low, cold cyan glow of a dual-monitor desktop rig flanked by matte black acoustic foam squares taped to cracked wallpaper.
  - Tangled sleeves of thick braided cables snake between empty aluminum taurine energy drink cans, instant ramyeon bowls crusted with dried chili oil, and spent blister packs of eye drops.
  - His interface consists of an unbadged heavy mechanical keyboard with customized linear switches and a high-DPI ergonomic gaming mouse polished smooth from millions of clicks.
- **The Stream Broadcast Context**:
  - Tae-Seok broadcasts under the handle **Classic Gamer Geek** (고전겜덕 / *Gojon-Gam-Deok*).
  - His stream is not an entertainment variety show; it is an endurance vigil. Viewers do not visit for light banter—they congregate like spectators at an execution ground to watch an obsessive tactician pit human sanity against mathematical impossibility.
  - The live viewer count rarely spikes during ordinary runs, but during deep defensive pushes, thousands of silent, breathless chat lurkers gather to witness the sheer geometric coldness of his micro-commands.

### 1.2 The Sadistic Masterpiece: "Protect the Empire" (제국을 지켜라)
- **Game Identity**: *Protect the Empire* is an infamous indie dark-fantasy tower defense, logistics management, and hero garrison simulation known colloquially among gaming communities as "The Sadist's Guillotine."
- **Global Completion Rate**: `0.000%`. Over six years since release, across hundreds of thousands of international players, no one had ever cleared the game on Hell Mode. The community consensus regarded the game as broken, spiteful shovelware designed by an algorithm that hates humanity.
- **Ludic Philosophy**:
  - The game simulates the desperate defense of the Everblack Empire’s southern boundary across 50 grueling stages.
  - Unlike modern tower defense games with respawning heroes, mana fountains, and generous checkpoint auto-saves, *Protect the Empire* models brutal medieval warfare with simulated systemic entropy. Masonry degrades under physical load, siege ballistas jam when soaked in acid rain, supply lines rot from mold, and hero morale deteriorates into psychosis under siege stress.

### 1.3 Hell Mode Ironman Permadeath Invariants
Hell Mode with Ironman restrictions represents the highest echelon of sadism programmed into the engine:
1. **Absolute Permadeath**:
   - Every hero unit, named knight, alchemical artificer, and common levy has a single life.
   - When their HP drops to `0`, their character data file is deleted from the active save block immediately. There are no resurrection scrolls, no divine revivification rituals, and no hospital miracles. Their equipment shatters, and their name is inscribed in a grim, static in-game cenotaph screen.
2. **Deterministic Irreversible Autosave**:
   - The game engine commits state directly to disk at the completion of every tick. Task-killing (`Alt+F4`), memory freezes, or save-file copying trigger an automated save-scrubbing hash validation failure that wipes the save directory. Every mistake, misplaced barricade, or delayed order is carved into stone permanently.
3. **Compound Resource Attrition**:
   - Repair granite, black powder, pitch oil, and food rations must be harvested from dangerous subterranean sallies.
   - The Imperial Capital sends zero military reinforcements. Every fallen soldier permanently shrinks the garrison roster.
4. **Unforgiving Spawn Randomization & Monster Synergies**:
   - Monster horde compositions mutate dynamically to exploit the player's specific roster weaknesses. If the player stacks heavy armor, the engine generates acid-spewing aerial monstrosities; if the player deploys agile archers, burrowing chitin horrors erupt beneath their battlements.

### 1.4 The 1056th Run — Three Years of Trauma
- **The Toll of Failure**:
  - Over three calendar years, Tae-Seok endured **1,055 consecutive catastrophic wipes**.
  - He has seen every character in the game die hundreds of times in every conceivable agony: burned alive by dragon breath, liquefied by corpse-worms, dissolved in acidic bile, crushed beneath falling gatehouses, and torn apart limb from limb by chittering arachnid hordes.
- **The Encyclopedic Trauma Mind**:
  - Tae-Seok does not play on reaction; he plays on trauma-induced clairvoyance.
  - He has memorized every frame of monster attack wind-ups, every damage hitbox, every pathfinding vector on the 30-meter basalt ramparts, every drop rate, and every dialogue bark of his roster.
  - His 1,056th attempt was different: through inhuman discipline, ruthless resource rationing, and sacrificial gambits, he dragged his battered garrison through 49 stages to stand before the impossible threshold: **Stage 50**.

## 2. Stage 50: The Final Wave & The Last Stand

### 2.1 The Citadel Ramparts Pulverized
- **The Environmental Apocalypse**:
  - Stage 50 takes place on the crumbling ramparts of the imperial inner bastion during an unrelenting tempest of freezing slanting rain and lightning.
  - The 30-meter monolithic curtain walls of volcanic basalt—standing proud for centuries—have been reduced to pulverized rubble, jagged granite stumps, and shattered battlements.
  - Defensive ballista towers have collapsed into burning timber piles; iron pitch cauldrons have tipped over, sending streams of flaming black naphtha sizzling across rain-slick flagstones.
  - The stone walkways are ankle-deep in a sickening slurry of rain, charcoal ash, dark crimson human blood, and phosphorescent black demonic ichor. Shattered pavises, twisted polearms, notched swords, and shredded imperial banners litter every square meter.

### 2.2 The Demonic Horde of the Monster Realm
- **The Apocalyptic Breach**:
  - The Black Fog, which normally lingers 300 meters beyond the fortress moat, has completely engulfed the battlefield in roiling tendrils of abyssal miasma.
  - From the abyss emerged the apocalyptic vanguard of the Monster Realm: titanic armored siege abominations, multi-limbed chitinous horrors, winged gargoyles spraying corrosive digestive acid, and demonic centurions wielding jagged obsidian cleavers.
  - The sheer volume of the horde was designed to crash any defensive permutation. The game engine had calculated this wave as mathematically unbreachable by human defense.

### 2.3 The Annihilation of the Hero Roster
- One by one, every legendary hero whom Tae-Seok had nurtured, geared, and positioned across 50 stages fell in brutal, unscripted sacrifices:
  - **Evangeline**, the indomitable paladin whose barrier arts had held the gatehouse for 40 stages, saw her divine aegis shatter under the hammer of an abyssal colossus before she was crushed beneath falling stone.
  - **Lily**, the blind sniper whose wind-guided arbalest bolts had pinned hundreds of flying terrors, fell when her sniping tower was severed by an acid volley, plunging into the black swarm below.
  - The royal battle-mages exhausted their mana cores until their eyes bled and their veins crystalized, collapsing as lifeless husks on the parapets.
  - The auxiliary levies, infantry spearmen, and alchemical engineers were ground into meat against the breached gatehouse.

### 2.4 Knight Lucas MacGregor: The Sole Surviving Vanguard Anchor
When the final boss—a mountain of writhing muscle, black bone plating, and multiple magma-veined blade arms—crawled over the breached ramparts, only one defender remained standing in the entire empire: **Knight Lucas MacGregor**.

Lucas was able to stand alone through the convergence of three foundational mechanics that made him the ultimate defensive cornerstone of the game:

1. **Passive Ability: *Man of Steel* (강철의 육체)**:
   - *Mechanic*: Provides colossal baseline physical and blunt trauma damage mitigation, scaling inversely with armor durability.
   - *The Invariant*: **Lethal Damage Nullification**. Once per battle, upon receiving an attack that would reduce his HP to 0 or inflict instant death, Lucas's HP is locked at `1 HP`. For a duration of 3 to 5 seconds, his body becomes an impenetrable living bulwark immune to stagger, knockback, or crowd control.
   - *Narrative Manifestation*: As the final boss unleashed an apocalyptic shockwave that vaporized the remaining garrison, Lucas absorbed the blast directly onto his spiked tower shield. His armor cracked, his tabard shredded, and his blood coated the masonry, but his boots remained dug into the stone at 1 HP.
2. **Core Active/Passive Skill: *Strike of Will* (의지의 일격)**:
   - *Mechanic*: An infinitely stacking offensive damage multiplier. For every enemy unit Lucas personally executes or parries with fatal intent during a continuous defensive battle, his physical attack power scales upward by an un-capped percentage.
   - *The Invariant*: In Stage 50, after standing as the vanguard anchor through hundreds of waves and personally slaying thousands of vanguard beasts, Lucas’s *Strike of Will* multiplier had compounded into catastrophic, god-slaying numbers. His single swing possessed more raw kinetic force than an imperial siege battery.
3. **Mobility & Intercept Skill: *Step of Tenacity* (집념의 일보)**:
   - *Mechanic*: An instantaneous, non-magical spatial intercept flash-step. When an enemy unit launches a lethal trajectory toward a designated inner defense zone or allied entity, Lucas compresses his muscular frame and launches across the battlefield at blinding speed, forcing his shield directly into the strike path.
   - *Narrative Manifestation*: When the boss launched its execution cleave toward the imperial throne room behind the gate, Lucas flashed across 40 meters of burning rubble, intercepting the blow with the iron boss of his shield before driving his bastard sword through the monster's core.

### 2.5 The Cold Geometric Mindset & Survivor's Guilt
- **Tae-Seok’s Input Trance**:
  - During the final 180 seconds of Stage 50, Tae-Seok’s Actions Per Minute (APM) spiked beyond `420`.
  - His mind operated in a state of hyper-rational, cold geometric calculation. There was no room for panic, empathy, or sentimentality. He calculated damage tick frequencies, animation recovery frames, and line-of-sight vectors with robotic precision.
  - He deliberately sacrificed retreating auxiliary spearmen to bait monster cleave cooldowns, buying the exact 2.8 seconds Lucas needed to recover stamina and align his *Step of Tenacity*.
- **The Empty Victory**:
  - With a final, thunderous diagonal slash of Lucas’s notched blade, the final boss bifurcated down its spinal ridge, collapsing into a heap of dissolving black ash.
  - The CRT monitor flashed violently:
    ```
    =======================================================
                   [ STAGE 50: FINAL STAGE ]
                   [ CONGRATULATIONS! CLEAR! ]
                   [ GLOBAL FIRST COMPLETION! ]
    =======================================================
    [ STAGE MVP ]: Lucas MacGregor (SSR) - Frontline Vanguard
    [ CASUALTIES ]: 99.8% (All units eliminated except Lucas)
    =======================================================
    ```
  - Outside, the chat room exploded in a deluge of celebratory emotes and donation alerts.
  - But inside the dark officetel, Tae-Seok did not cheer. His hands trembled on the mouse. He stared at the monitor where Lucas stood alone in the rain among the corpses of all his comrades, his battered head bowed in silent mourning.
  - Tae-Seok slumped forward, resting his forehead against the cold edge of his desk, weeping quietly:
    *"We won... but at what cost? Everyone is dead. Only Lucas is left."*

## 3. Master Character Profile: Knight Lucas MacGregor

### 3.1 Lineage, Honor, and Archetype
- **Full Canonical Name & Titles**:
  - Lucas MacGregor (루카스 맥그리거)
  - Vanguard Knight of House MacGregor
  - Escort Knight to the 36th Imperial Prince Ash Everblack
  - Later revered across the continent as **The Night Closer** (밤을 닫는 자 / *The One Who Shuts the Night*)
- **Lineage & Standing**:
  - Born to House MacGregor, an ancient frontier knightly house whose ancestral motto is: *"The Shield Never Sleeps."* Unlike the decadent, sycophantic nobility of New Terra, House MacGregor has guarded the southern borders for generations.
  - Assigned as personal escort knight to Prince Ash Everblack as a political punishment: the Imperial Court banished Lucas to the Southern Front under the guise of an honor guard, tying his fate to the empire’s most despised royal exile.
- **Archetype & Core Psychology**:
  - *The Immovable Shield / The Tragic Loyalist*: Lucas represents the purest ideal of chivalric duty. He does not seek fame, gold, or political advancement. His loyalty is unconditioned by the character of his master; he protects Ash simply because an escort knight protects his lord.
  - *The "Golden Retriever Knight"*: Beneath his battle-hardened exterior and scarred flesh lies an earnest, deeply compassionate heart. He is candid, incapable of political deceit, and forms fierce, protective bonds with those under his shield.
  - *Chronic Survivor's Guilt*: Lucas suffers from a profound internal wound: he believes it is an unforgivable sin for any comrade, levy, or civilian to die while he remains standing. He throws his body into lethal monster strikes not out of a death wish, but out of a desperate conviction that his shield must be the final barrier.

### 3.2 Physical Anatomy, Facial Scars & Grounded Expression
- **Age & Physiological Build**:
  - Age: 22 to 25 during Stage 0/1 (the Prologue and Crossroads Tutorial), aging into a rugged, battle-worn veteran across the campaign.
  - Height & Stature: ~192 cm (6'3.5"). Heavy muscular powerhouse frame with wide, imposing clavicles, dense trapezius muscles, and broad shoulders engineered to absorb the shock of charging beasts.
- **Cranial & Facial Architecture**:
  - Square, heavily chiseled masculine bone structure with a wide mandibular angle and a broad chin featuring a subtle vertical cleft.
  - Heavy brow ridge casting deep ambient occlusion shadows over the eye sockets, with pronounced vertical furrows (corrugator supercilii) carved between the brows from endless vigilance.
- **Scars & Combat Trauma**:
  - *The Left Cheek Claw Scar*: A signature three-furrow healed keloid claw scar (#B88A8A) running diagonally from his left high cheekbone across his jawline, inflicted by a ghoul stalker in his first campaign.
  - *Fresh Battle Trauma*: A 25mm laceration along his right temple hairline oozing a sluggish trickle of dark venous blood (#4A0E17) that mixes with rainwater and runs down his right jaw plane.
  - *Skin Texture*: Coarse, weathered bronze-tan skin (#C29672) with visible pores, rain droplets glistening on the cheekbones, and charcoal soot smudges (#222226) across his forehead.
- **Hair**:
  - Dirty-blonde / honey-amber (#C49A45 base, #5A401E shadow crevices, #E8C170 highlight glints), heavily dulled by soot, rainwater, and dried blood.
  - Layered, shaggy cut falling to chin length in front and nape length in back; heavy, wet clumps whip across his brow in the wind.

### 3.3 Visual Grounding: The 3/4 Heroic Battle Roar Framing (Zero Arcade Sparks)
To honor the core creative mandate and eliminate cartoonish high-fantasy clichés:
- **Strict Prohibition on Luminescent Tropes**:
  - Zero glowing eye sparks, zero laser pupils, zero electric blue lightning arcs around his armor, and zero flaming magical runes on his blade.
- **Anatomically Grounded Human Eyes**:
  - Natural weathered steel-gray irises (#4A5A66 outer limbal ring, #708494 inner stroma) with subtle slate-blue undertones.
  - Realistic dual-specular catchlights: a warm flickering amber glint at the 10 o'clock position (reflecting rampart pitch braziers) and a sharp, cold slate-blue rim highlight at 4 o'clock (reflecting the twilight storm horizon).
  - Bloodshot micro-capillary engorgement in the sclera from 48 hours of continuous smoke inhalation, cold wind, and sleep deprivation.
- **Dynamic 3/4 Perspective & Camera Mechanics**:
  - Framed at a dynamic three-quarter low-angle perspective (`Yaw 25-35 deg`, `Pitch +8 to +12 deg`, `Dutch tilt 10-14 deg`) pushing in 15% along the Z-axis over 4.0 seconds.
  - Lucas's head is tilted slightly forward, brow clamped hard, jaw unhinging from a tense clench into an authoritative, guttural command roar ("CHARGE! / FORWARD!").
  - Fine condensation breath vapor and saliva mist burst into the freezing rain as his masseter and platysma muscles bulge with effort.
- **Over-the-Shoulder Vanguard Staging**:
  - Lucas is not framed in isolation: over his left shoulder in deep cinematic bokeh (`f/2.2`), the blurred silhouettes of three charging vanguard infantrymen with raised iron spears and burning torches surge forward into the breach, establishing him as the roaring apex of an advancing battle line.

### 3.4 Armor Metallurgy & Textile Heraldry

  - Hand-forged 15th-century gothic plate steel with an oiled matte gunmetal finish (#26292E base, #484E58 specular edges, #121316 ambient occlusion crevices). Zero mirror chrome or clean parade gloss.
  - Surface Patina: Two deep, parallel claw gouges (3mm depth) torn across the left breastplate and left pauldron, exposing raw, unoxidized silver-white steel (#D8DDE6) with raised metal burrs. Mottled black acid-etching on the gorget from gargoyle venom, and concave hammer dents along the breastplate plackart.
  - Fasteners: Hand-hammered domed iron rivets with faint rings of rust (#5C3826); dark brown bridle leather straps (#3A2418) with iron wire-sewn buckles and frayed strap ends.
  - Underlayers: Riveted round-wire iron maille hauberk visible at the neck hollow beneath the gorget and along the armpit voids.
- **Heraldic Tabard**:
  - Heavy coarse-weave wool surcoat dyed faded imperial navy (#1E2D48), split down front and back.
  - Emblazoned with the House MacGregor heraldic lion paw and the Crossroads fortress tower crest—blackened by soot, frayed at the hem, and stained with dark dried blood.

### 3.5 Weapons & Wielding Style
- **Primary Bastard Sword (Hand-and-a-Half)**:
  - Oakeshott Type XVIIIb longsword (~90 cm high-carbon steel blade, diamond cross-section, central fuller extending down the proximal third).
  - The blade shows fine longitudinal grinding lines, rain beads tracking along the fuller, and four distinct micro-notches on the cutting edges where it struck monster bone and iron armor.
  - Hilt: Blackened steel crossguard with downturned octagonal quillons, hexagonal scent-stopper pommel, and oxblood cord-wrapped leather grip (#2A1816) worn smooth where the gauntlets rested.
  - Zero-Glow Rule: Reflections on the blade are purely specular bounces of physical torchlight (#E67E22) and storm moonlight (#7F9AB5).
- **Spiked Tower Shield / Pavise**:
  - Massive iron-reinforced oak pavise (~140 cm tall, 65 cm wide) with a heavy iron boss and hardened lower ground-spikes designed to bite into stone masonry to resist the charge of multi-ton beasts.
  - Deep vertical claw gouges score the face of the shield down to the splintered wood beneath.
- **Fighting Style**:
  - Rooted, kinetic, earth-shaking swordcraft. Lucas does not perform acrobatic spins; he plants his back foot, catches charging beasts on the angled plane of his shield, channels the kinetic impact into the ground, and steps forward with devastating, bone-shattering counter-cleaves.

### 3.6 Signature Dialogue & Martial Philosophy

- *"As long as I draw breath, not a single beast shall set foot upon this wall!"*

- *"I am the shield of House MacGregor. A shield does not retreat."*

## 4. The Transmigration: Awakening as Prince Ash Bornhater

### 4.1 The Transition & The Drafty Citadel Chamber
- **The Threshold of Consciousness**:
  - Following the clearing of Stage 50, Lee Tae-Seok collapsed onto his keyboard in total physiological and emotional exhaustion. The harsh hum of his PC cooling fans, the rhythmic clicking of his mechanical switches, and the blue glow of his CRT monitor dissolved into a suffocating, weightless void.
  - He woke not to the familiar electronic hum of his Seoul officetel, but to an overwhelming sensory assault:
    - The biting, humid draft of subterranean mountain air whistling through unglazed arrow slits.
    - The pungent, organic stench of tallow candles, damp moldy granite, horse manure from the lower courtyards, and faint sulfur rising from volcanic fissures.
- **The Physical Setting**:
  - Tae-Seok opened his eyes inside a cavernous, dilapidated stone bedchamber inside the Lord’s Keep of Crossroads Fortress.
  - He lay upon a grand yet moth-eaten four-poster bed draped in faded imperial crimson velvet. Across the room, a massive stone fireplace held dying peat embers.
  - Stumbling across the freezing flagstones, he stood before a tall, cracked silvered mirror mounted on iron brackets. The reflection looking back was not the doughy, sleep-deprived face of an exhausted 23-year-old Korean gamer, but the sharp, aristocratic countenance of a despised imperial prince.

### 4.2 Vessel Profile: Prince Ash "The Born Hater" Everblack
- **Full Imperial Title**:
  - Ash Bornhater Everblack (애쉬 본 헤이터 에버블랙)
  - 36th Imperial Prince of the Everblack Empire
  - Lord Commander of the Southern Frontier Outpost of Crossroads
- **Imperial Reputation & Notoriety**:
  - Known throughout the grand salons of the capital New Terra as **The Trash Prince** and **The Born Hater**.
  - Born with a vicious, sardonic tongue, chronic paranoia, and zero aptitude for imperial martial magic, Ash had made enemies of nearly all 35 of his royal half-siblings.
  - His father, the Emperor, viewed him as an embarrassment to the dragon bloodline. Under the pretext of a promotion to "Commander of the Southern Bastion," Ash was formally exiled to Crossroads—a notorious penal dumping ground where disgraced nobles and convict levies were expected to be slaughtered by the first serious monster surge.
- **Physical Features & Aesthetic Invariants**:
  - Stature: Tall, lean, aristocratic silhouette (~182 cm, 5'11.5").
  - Complexion: Unhealthily pale skin contrasting sharply with pitch-black, disheveled hair that falls loosely over his forehead and temples.
  - Facial Anatomy: Sharp, hawkish jawline, thin mocking lips, and piercing amber-gold eyes (#D4AF37) that gleam with calculating, predatory intensity beneath deep, bruised-purple insomnia rings.
  - Attire: A tailored charcoal-black officer greatcoat with frayed gold bullion embroidery along the high collar, dark crimson silk lining, a scorched iron gorget, polished black leather cavalry boots, and an uncut raw obsidian signet ring on his right hand.

### 4.3 The First Encounter: The Unawakened Knight and the Disgraced Prince
- **The Meeting at the Chamber Door**:
  - Gripping his pounding temples, Ash pushed open the heavy oak door of his bedchamber. Standing vigil in the corridor, leaning against the cold masonry, was **Knight Lucas MacGregor**.
  - The encounter is layered with profound tragic irony:
    - *Lucas’s Perspective*: Lucas sees only Prince Ash—the vile, cowardly royal exile whose debauchery and insults have disgraced the fortress. Lucas stands guard solely out of chivalric duty to House MacGregor’s oath. His stance is rigid, cold, and heavy with thinly veiled contempt.
    - *Ash’s / Tae-Seok’s Perspective*: To the gamer, the young man standing before him is not an insolent subordinate—he is the holy companion of 1,056 campaigns. He is the warrior who took thousands of fatal blows, who stood alone at the gatehouse in Stage 50, who cleaved the final boss in half while bleeding from every pore.
- **The Gamer's Diagnostic Shock**:
  - Looking at Lucas, Tae-Seok’s gamer vision instinctively registers his status window:
    - Level: `1`.
    - Rank: `SSR` (Base Unawakened).
    - Traits: *Man of Steel* (Dormant), *Strike of Will* (Locked), *Step of Tenacity* (Unpracticed).
    - Gear: Standard battered guard plate, dull iron broadsword, plain wooden pavise.
  - Tae-Seok realizes the agonizing truth: this is not his god-slaying Stage 50 Lucas. This is a young, unawakened greenhorn knight who will die in his first skirmish if commanded incorrectly.

### 4.4 The Horn of Stage 0: The Tutorial Wave Crisis
- **The Shriek of the War Horns**:
  - Before Ash can speak, the silence of the fortress is shattered by the deafening wail of the bronze siren bells and brass war horns from the outer parapets:
    ```
    [ EMERGENCY ALERT: THE BLACK FOG HAS EXPANDED ]
    [ TIER-1 INVASION DETECTED: THE SOUTHERN BREACH ]
    [ TUTORIAL STAGE 0: REPEL THE PROWLERS ]
    ```
  - Outside the windows, the sky turns blood-purple. Black chitinous arachnid horrors (Black Crawlers and Corpse Scavengers) have scaled the outer ditch and are pouring through the drainage culverts into the middle district.
  - Panic erupts: conscript levies drop their pikes and flee, drunk mercenaries bar themselves in taverns, and junior officers abandon their posts in terror.
- **The Weight of Living Flesh**:
  - In an instant, the simulation vanishes. The icy mountain wind freezes his cheeks; the sulfur in the air burns his lungs; the ground shakes under the scuttling claws of monsters.
  - Permadeath is no longer a line of code deleting a save file—it is the physical reality of human bodies being crushed and eaten alive. If Lucas falls here, there is no restart button. There is no 1,057th attempt.

### 4.5 The Commander's Rebirth: Gamer Instinct in the Living Flesh
- **Shedding the Gamer, Becoming the Tyrant**:
  - Tae-Seok suppresses his rising nausea and leans entirely into the terrifying persona of **Prince Ash Bornhater**.
  - He understands that on this brutal frontier, kindness and hesitation invite slaughter. Conscripts do not listen to gentle requests—they respond to a commander more terrifying than the monsters.
  - Drawing on his encyclopedic mastery of monster pathfinding, spawn cadences, and choke-point geometry, Ash barks out sharp, razor-precise orders that shock Lucas out of his paralysis:
    *"Draw your blade, Knight Lucas! If you want to die like a coward, step aside. If you want to live, put your shield on that doorway and do not move an inch until I tell you to breathe!"*
  - Recognizing the absolute tactical certainty in Ash’s voice, Lucas’s eyes widen in disbelief—and for the first time, he drops his pavise into the flagstones and draws his sword.
  - The gamer has taken the field. The defense of the empire has begun in blood.
